UPGRADE THE EXISTING BEADERA WEBSITE.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT rebuild the website from scratch.
Do NOT replace the existing product system, admin panel, database, routing, or working functionality.

First inspect the ENTIRE existing codebase and understand the current design, components, product system, navigation, admin functionality and responsive behavior.

Then upgrade the existing website while preserving everything that already works.

The goal is to make Beadera feel like a real premium handmade lifestyle brand, not a generic e-commerce website.

==================================================
CORE BRAND IDEA
==================================================

BEADERA is not simply a bead accessories store.

We sell:

EMOTIONS.
MEMORIES.
CREATIVITY.
CONNECTION.
EXPERIENCES.

Our strongest USP is our WORKSHOP EXPERIENCE.

Customers don't only have to purchase something from Beadera.

They can come to a Beadera workshop, choose their own beads, learn from us, create something with their own hands and then gift that handmade creation to someone they love.

This emotional concept needs to become one of the strongest visual moments on the website.

The website should communicate:

"You could buy a gift.

Or...

you could make the gift."
