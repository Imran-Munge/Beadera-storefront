Build a premium, responsive, single-page e-commerce website for a handmade bead accessories brand called:

BEADERA

Brand idea:
"We don't just sell accessories. We create emotions."

Beadera creates handmade bead-based accessories and gifts. The brand is built around emotions, relationships, memories and personal expression.

IMPORTANT:
Use the attached Beadera logo as the primary brand reference.
Do NOT redesign the logo.
Do NOT use generic jewellery/e-commerce visuals.
Do NOT make the website look like a template.
Do NOT copy TinyMinyMo or any other website directly.

The website can take UX inspiration from modern product collection websites:
- clean product grids
- large product photography
- simple product information
- minimal navigation
- strong visual hierarchy
But the visual identity must be completely Beadera-specific.
